# BU1 v2 — Brand Core

Status: **draft**

## 1. Brand character

BU1 v2 should feel:

- confident,
- specialist,
- modern,
- technical,
- athletic,
- visually cultured,
- premium without becoming distant.

BU1 should not feel like a generic sporting-goods marketplace. It should feel like a focused goalkeeper brand with a recognizable visual world.

## 2. Brand promise in design

The design should communicate three things immediately:

1. **This product matters.** It is shown clearly, confidently and at meaningful scale.
2. **We know goalkeeping.** Technical proof is concise and specific.
3. **BU1 has its own point of view.** The composition feels authored, not templated.

## 3. Core visual idea

**Scene for one hero.**

The interface, poster, package or ad acts as a stage. The product is the hero. Typography is the second dominant element. Everything else is support.

This does not mean empty or sterile design. It means controlled hierarchy:

> hero product → hero typography → proof → commerce action → utilities

## 4. Non-negotiables

### Exact product

For any real BU1 product:

- preserve real colors,
- preserve pattern and material details,
- preserve logos and marks,
- preserve silhouette and proportions,
- do not hallucinate product features,
- do not use prompt-only generation when product fidelity matters.

Final commerce and paid-media assets should use exact product photography or a controlled exact-product composite.

### Exact brand marks

- Use official BU1 logo assets.
- Never regenerate the logo with an image model.
- Never approximate the logo with text or a similar symbol.
- Do not introduce unofficial marks merely to fill space.

### Product-led color

The product itself is usually the main color event in the composition.

Examples:

- MiKos → cyan/blue details naturally become the campaign accent.
- Another glove may lead with orange, green, red, neutral, or another product color.

This is intentional. BU1 v2 is not a system where every asset must be covered in the same brand accent.

### Hot Rose

Hot Rose is a semantic brand signal, not a decorative fill.

Recommended uses:

- NOVINKA
- SALE / VÝPRODEJ
- DROP
- LIMITED
- urgency or active promotional state
- small active UI signal where appropriate

Avoid using it simply because an empty area needs color.

## 5. Tone of voice

Copy should be:

- short,
- direct,
- concrete,
- specific,
- confident,
- grounded in real product or athlete truth.

Avoid generic marketing filler such as:

- revolutionary,
- ultimate,
- game-changing,
- premium quality,
- best ever,
- unique technology,

unless the statement is supported by real evidence and is genuinely useful.

## 6. Preferred copy architecture

A typical launch asset should contain only what it needs:

- semantic label: `NOVINKA`
- hero name or claim: `MIKOS.`
- support line: `LEHKOST. CIT. JISTOTA.`
- proof: `Contact Grip · NC střih · 2 odnímatelné pásky`
- price if relevant
- CTA if relevant

Not every asset needs every layer.

## 7. Relationship between brand and channel

The core brand stays stable. The expression may change by context.

- Web can be quieter and more editorial.
- Social can use stronger crops and scale contrast.
- PPC can reduce the system to one message and one product.
- Print can exploit physical scale and whitespace.
- Packaging can be even more restrained.
- Video can introduce movement, rhythm, sound and real goalkeeper action.

Consistency comes from the system’s rules, not from making every asset look identical.
