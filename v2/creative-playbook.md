# BU1 v2 — Creative Playbook

Status: **draft / canonical within v2**

This playbook translates the BU1 v2 brand direction into channel-specific execution rules. The system should feel consistent without forcing every channel into one layout.

## 1. Universal creative rules

Every BU1 v2 asset should start from these questions:

1. What is the single hero?
2. What is the single primary message?
3. What exact product / athlete truth supports it?
4. Which channel constraints matter?
5. Which visual mechanism best expresses the idea?

### Universal hierarchy

```text
hero product / athlete
→ hero typography
→ proof
→ price / offer
→ CTA / utility
```

### Universal limits

Prefer:

- 1 hero,
- 1 primary headline,
- 1 support line,
- 1–3 proof points,
- 1 action.

Avoid stacking every available fact into one asset.

---

## 2. Web / storefront

### Goal

Premium editorial commerce with strong product clarity and high conversion usability.

### Default mode

**Light Editorial**.

### Hero anatomy

Recommended elements:

- exact BU1 logo in header,
- editorial navigation,
- semantic label if relevant,
- short product / campaign headline,
- one support line,
- price when useful,
- one concise proof block,
- clear CTA,
- oversized exact product.

### Composition

- product may occupy 45–65% of viewport width,
- allow intentional crop at top / right / bottom,
- headline may occupy 30–45% of visible area,
- use generous breathing room,
- keep utilities visually quiet,
- avoid card-heavy hero composition.

### Copy

Good:

- `MIKOS.`
- `LEHKOST. CIT. JISTOTA.`
- `Contact Grip · NC střih · 2 odnímatelné pásky`

Avoid long product paragraphs inside the hero.

### Mobile

Do not simply scale desktop down.

Recompose:

- headline first or product first depending on product silhouette,
- preserve safe areas,
- reduce proof points,
- avoid overly tall stacked copy,
- keep CTA reachable and legible.

---

## 3. Social feed — Instagram / Facebook

### Goal

Stop scroll immediately while preserving premium BU1 identity.

### Preferred formats

- 4:5 primary,
- 1:1 secondary / reuse.

### Rules

- fewer words than web,
- larger crop than web,
- headline readable in under one second,
- CTA optional if platform CTA exists,
- product must stay recognizable at small mobile size,
- use more experimental mechanisms than storefront.

### Recommended creative families

#### A. Oversized product poster

```text
NOVINKA
MIKOS.
LEHKOST. CIT. JISTOTA.
[large exact product]
```

Best for launch / awareness.

#### B. Ghost word

Large low-contrast `MIKOS` / `BU1` layer behind product.

Best for editorial campaign feel.

#### C. Technical proof

```text
MIKOS.
CONTACT GRIP.
NC STŘIH · 2 PÁSKY
```

Best for benefit-led creative.

#### D. Athlete collaboration

Real athlete + exact product with one collaboration proof.

Best for credibility.

#### E. Dark product portrait

Black / charcoal foundation, white hero type, dramatic exact product crop.

Best for contrast in feed rotations.

### Feed density rule

Do not show all of these at once:

- price,
- 3 benefits,
- collaboration,
- CTA,
- sizes,
- offer,
- long description.

Choose the minimum required to express the hypothesis.

---

## 4. Stories / Reels covers

### Goal

Maximum immediate mobile impact.

### Format

9:16 designed natively.

### Safe-zone behavior

- keep important type away from top platform UI,
- keep CTA / price above lower interaction UI,
- crop product confidently but preserve recognition.

### Default hierarchy

```text
semantic label
hero headline
hero product
price / one proof
CTA
```

### Recommended mode

Use **Dark Impact** more often than on feed, but rotate with Light Editorial.

### Good mechanisms

- product rising from bottom edge,
- extreme macro crop,
- giant word above product,
- thin directional line,
- reveal-style frame designed to animate later.

---

## 5. Paid social / Meta PPC

### Goal

A clear, measurable conversion or attention hypothesis.

### Rule

**One concept = one reason to care.**

Examples:

- product launch,
- lightness,
- grip,
- athlete proof,
- price / offer,
- fit / cut.

### Creative test matrix

Test major variables independently when possible:

- Light Editorial vs Dark Impact,
- whole product vs macro proof,
- athlete vs product only,
- `MIKOS.` vs benefit-led headline,
- price visible vs hidden,
- CTA embedded vs platform CTA only,
- static clean vs stronger motion / texture.

### Copy density

Paid-social static should usually contain:

- product / benefit headline,
- optional semantic label,
- one proof or price,
- optional CTA.

The ad caption can carry the rest.

### Performance metadata

Every concept should store:

- concept ID,
- visual mechanism ID,
- product,
- audience,
- hypothesis,
- format,
- copy variant,
- result.

---

## 6. Google Display / PMax

### Goal

Brand recognition under aggressive automated cropping.

### Do not build one master asset

Prepare a family:

1. exact product only,
2. product + short headline,
3. product + price,
4. product + one proof,
5. Light Editorial,
6. Dark Impact.

### Rules

- do not depend on tiny embedded microcopy,
- keep headline short,
- leave crop-safe negative space,
- ensure product remains identifiable in landscape and square placements.

---

## 7. Print

### Goal

Physical scale, premium restraint and distance readability.

### Use

- oversized photography,
- one major statement,
- large margins,
- technical annotation as an editorial layer,
- excellent source resolution,
- material / finish as part of the design.

### Applications

- A2 / A3 posters,
- retail signage,
- launch cards,
- B2B showroom sheets,
- event graphics,
- athlete cards.

### Avoid

Turning a poster into a dense catalogue page.

---

## 8. Packaging

### Goal

Recognizable shelf / unboxing identity with controlled information hierarchy.

### Front

Prefer:

- BU1 brand,
- model / range,
- monochrome foundation,
- one product-led accent or identifying mark,
- minimal semantic label.

### Side / back

Place:

- technical specifications,
- size,
- material,
- care,
- legal information,
- QR / support,
- product story where useful.

### Rule

Packaging should feel like part of the same editorial system, not like a mini PDP.

---

## 9. Product graphics

### Goal

Make the object itself unmistakably BU1 while protecting function and long-term recognizability.

### Rules

- function / panel construction first,
- graphic hierarchy must work at distance,
- accent belongs to the product concept,
- permanent product graphics should age better than campaign graphics,
- Hot Rose is not a mandatory product color,
- do not over-decorate every available panel.

### Product-system opportunity

Consider defining consistent locations / behaviors for:

- BU1 mark,
- model identifier,
- foam / cut technology cue,
- signature / athlete collaboration cue,
- accent panel behavior.

---

## 10. Retail / POS

### Goal

Fast product recognition in a noisy physical environment.

### Rules

- one large product,
- one headline,
- one price / proof,
- strong distance readability,
- maintain family consistency across shelf strips, posters and stands.

Use Dark Impact selectively for hero moments.

---

## 11. Email

### Goal

Campaign clarity without reproducing the whole website inside the email.

### Structure

- one opening hero,
- short headline,
- one CTA,
- compact proof,
- supporting product modules below.

### Rule

The email hero can reuse the campaign mechanism but should not contain storefront navigation inside the image.

---

## 12. Video / motion

### Goal

Translate BU1 editorial performance into time.

### Production priority

1. real product footage,
2. real athlete interaction,
3. controlled composites,
4. AI extensions / environments,
5. fully generated non-product concept shots where useful.

### Recommended shot grammar

```text
HOOK
→ MACRO PROOF
→ FIT / PREPARATION
→ ACTION
→ PRODUCT HERO
→ END FRAME
```

### Example 10–12 s launch

1. 0–1.5 s — macro MiKos pattern / grip,
2. 1.5–3 s — strap / fit action,
3. 3–5 s — glove in ready position,
4. 5–8 s — save / grip moment,
5. 8–10 s — freeze / exact product hero,
6. 10–12 s — `MIKOS.` + short CTA.

### Motion typography

- use the same display hierarchy as static,
- animate scale / crop before inventing flashy effects,
- use hard cuts, controlled tracking and freeze frames,
- avoid generic particle explosions unless the concept specifically calls for them.

---

## 13. Photography direction

### Product studio

Prefer:

- clean directional light,
- strong material readability,
- clear silhouette,
- real color,
- high-resolution exact assets,
- both whole-product and macro-detail coverage.

### Athlete

Capture:

- preparation,
- hand / strap detail,
- ready position,
- save moments,
- ball contact,
- post-action calm,
- portrait with product visible.

### Required launch set

For major products, aim to have:

- isolated front / back / palm views,
- 2–3 macro technical details,
- one strong editorial still,
- one athlete product interaction,
- vertical action footage,
- horizontal action footage.

This reduces dependence on AI for product-critical work.

---

## 14. Creative QA by channel

Before publish, check:

### Brand

- exact product?
- exact logo if shown?
- product-led color?
- Hot Rose semantic?
- typography consistent?

### Composition

- one hero?
- authored asymmetry?
- enough negative space?
- product large enough?
- microcopy genuinely needed?

### Channel

- correct aspect ratio?
- safe zones?
- readable on mobile / at distance?
- platform UI conflict?
- automated crop risk?

### Commerce

- price current?
- claims true?
- CTA appropriate?
- no invented technology?

---

## 15. Recommended starting matrix for a product launch

For every major launch, produce at minimum:

| Asset | Format | Mode | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Storefront hero | 16:9 / responsive | Light Editorial | oversized product |
| Social feed A | 4:5 | Light Editorial | product + short claim |
| Social feed B | 4:5 | Light Editorial | ghost word |
| Social feed C | 4:5 | Dark Impact | dark product portrait |
| Story | 9:16 | Dark Impact | product + CTA |
| PPC square | 1:1 | Light Editorial | one benefit |
| PPC alternative | 1:1 | Dark Impact | one benefit |
| PMax clean | flexible | neutral | product only |
| Print poster | A2/A3 | Light or Dark | oversized product |

The launch should feel like one campaign family without becoming one resized master layout.
